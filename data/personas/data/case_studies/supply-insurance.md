# Supply-chain reporting for a insurance company

## Problem
The client, a insurance business, wanted to track deliveries and stock in one report. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a supply-chain reporting using Power BI and SQL Server. DAX covered the heavy lifting, and SSIS tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work reduced late-shipment reporting lag from 5 days to 1.
