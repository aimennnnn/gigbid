# Sales forecasting workbook for a telecom company

## Problem
The client, a telecom business, wanted to estimate next quarter's sales. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a sales forecasting workbook using Python and Prophet. Excel covered the heavy lifting, and time series tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work landed within 4% of actual revenue for two quarters.
