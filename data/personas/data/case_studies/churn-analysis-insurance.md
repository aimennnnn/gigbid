# Churn driver analysis for a insurance company

## Problem
The client, a insurance business, wanted to explain why customers cancel. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a churn driver analysis using SQL and Python. survival analysis covered the heavy lifting, and pandas tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work showed onboarding delay explained most early churn.
