# Data cleaning and deduplication for a SaaS company

## Problem
The client, a SaaS business, wanted to tidy up our messy customer list. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a data cleaning and deduplication using Python and pandas. fuzzy matching covered the heavy lifting, and SQL tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work merged 38,000 duplicate customer records.
