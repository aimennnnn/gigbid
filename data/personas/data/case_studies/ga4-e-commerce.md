# Ga4 and web analytics reporting for a e-commerce company

## Problem
The client, a e-commerce business, wanted to understand what visitors do on our site. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a GA4 and web analytics reporting using GA4 and BigQuery. Looker Studio covered the heavy lifting, and SQL tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work found a checkout step losing 41% of mobile users.
