# Looker studio reporting suite for a insurance company

## Problem
The client, a insurance business, wanted to simple shareable reports for our clients. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a Looker Studio reporting suite using Looker Studio and Google Sheets. SQL covered the heavy lifting, and BigQuery tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work rolled out to 25 client accounts from one template.
