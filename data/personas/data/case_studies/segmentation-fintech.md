# Customer segmentation for a fintech company

## Problem
The client, a fintech business, wanted to group our customers so we can market to them differently. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a customer segmentation using Python and k-means. RFM analysis covered the heavy lifting, and pandas tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work identified 5 segments; the top one drove 46% of revenue.
