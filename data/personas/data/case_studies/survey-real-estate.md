# Survey analysis and reporting for a real estate company

## Problem
The client, a real estate business, wanted to make sense of our customer survey. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a survey analysis and reporting using R and statistics. ggplot2 covered the heavy lifting, and survey weighting tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work weighted 4,200 responses and found three drivers of satisfaction.
