# Pricing and margin analysis for a insurance company

## Problem
The client, a insurance business, wanted to see where we are losing margin. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a pricing and margin analysis using SQL and Excel. Python covered the heavy lifting, and regression tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work identified 60 SKUs sold below cost.
