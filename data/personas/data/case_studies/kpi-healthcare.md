# Kpi framework and metric definitions for a healthcare company

## Problem
The client, a healthcare business, wanted to agree what we measure and how. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a KPI framework and metric definitions using SQL and metric design. Looker covered the heavy lifting, and stakeholder interviews tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work aligned 4 teams on 12 shared metric definitions.
