# Marketing attribution analysis for a retail company

## Problem
The client, a retail business, wanted to work out which ads actually bring sales. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a marketing attribution analysis using SQL and Python. GA4 covered the heavy lifting, and attribution modelling tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work moved 30% of spend and lowered cost per acquisition by 17%.
