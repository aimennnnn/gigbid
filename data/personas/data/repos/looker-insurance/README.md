# looker-insurance

Internal tooling built for a insurance client. The goal was simple: simple shareable reports for our clients.

## Layout
- `src/` main pipeline
- `tests/` checks on the outputs
- `notebooks/` exploration, not used in production

## Notes
Outcome after rollout: rolled out to 25 client accounts from one template. Depends on `looker-studio` and `gspread`.
