# Sensor anomaly detection for a SaaS company

## Problem
The client, a SaaS business, wanted to warn us when a machine starts behaving oddly. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a sensor anomaly detection using Python and Isolation Forest. time series covered the heavy lifting, and Kafka tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work flagged 9 of 10 real failures a median of 14 hours early.
