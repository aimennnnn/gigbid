# Semantic search over a product catalogue for a fintech company

## Problem
The client, a fintech business, wanted to let people search by meaning instead of exact words. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a semantic search over a product catalogue using sentence-transformers and FAISS. Elasticsearch covered the heavy lifting, and Python tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work reduced zero-result searches from 18% to 5%.
