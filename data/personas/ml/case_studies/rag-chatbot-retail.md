# Retrieval-augmented support chatbot for a retail company

## Problem
The client, a retail business, wanted to a bot that answers customer questions from our help articles. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a retrieval-augmented support chatbot using LangChain and RAG. Pinecone covered the heavy lifting, and FastAPI tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work deflected 38% of tier-1 support tickets in the first month.
