# Invoice ocr and extraction pipeline for a healthcare company

## Problem
The client, a healthcare business, wanted to read scanned invoices and pull out the numbers. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a invoice OCR and extraction pipeline using Tesseract and LayoutLM. Python covered the heavy lifting, and OpenCV tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work extracted line items with 97% field accuracy on 5,000 invoices.
