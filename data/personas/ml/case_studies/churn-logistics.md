# Customer churn prediction model for a logistics company

## Problem
The client, a logistics business, wanted to figure out which customers are about to leave. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a customer churn prediction model using Python and scikit-learn. XGBoost covered the heavy lifting, and pandas tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work lifted recall on churning accounts from 41% to 73% at the same precision.
