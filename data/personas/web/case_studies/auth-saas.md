# Authentication and single sign-on for a SaaS company

## Problem
The client, a SaaS business, wanted to let staff sign in with their company account. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a authentication and single sign-on using OAuth 2.0 and JWT. Auth0 covered the heavy lifting, and Node.js tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work removed 300 password-reset tickets a month.
