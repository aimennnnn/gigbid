# Payment and subscription integration for a fintech company

## Problem
The client, a fintech business, wanted to take monthly payments and handle failed cards. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a payment and subscription integration using Stripe and webhooks. Node.js covered the heavy lifting, and TypeScript tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work recovered 23% of failed renewals with retry logic.
