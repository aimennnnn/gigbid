# Two-sided marketplace for a real estate company

## Problem
The client, a real estate business, wanted to a site where sellers list and buyers pay, and we take a cut. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a two-sided marketplace using React and Node.js. Stripe Connect covered the heavy lifting, and PostgreSQL tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work processed $410k in payouts in the first quarter.
