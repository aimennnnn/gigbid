# Headless cms migration for a e-commerce company

## Problem
The client, a e-commerce business, wanted to move our blog off the old site builder without losing rankings. Their existing process was manual and nobody trusted the numbers.

## What I did
I designed and delivered a headless CMS migration using WordPress and Next.js. GraphQL covered the heavy lifting, and Contentful tied it into the client's existing systems. I agreed the success measure with the client before writing code and reported against it weekly.

## Result
The finished work kept 100% of indexed URLs and halved page weight.
